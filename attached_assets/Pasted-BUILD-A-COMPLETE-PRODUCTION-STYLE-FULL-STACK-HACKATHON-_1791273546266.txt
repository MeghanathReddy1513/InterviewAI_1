==================================================

When Start Interview is clicked:

1. Create interview session in Supabase.
2. Save all configuration.
3. Generate relevant questions.
4. Show one question at a time.
5. Evaluate every answer.
6. Adapt the next question.
7. Save everything.
8. Generate final report.

- Selected role
- Interview type
- Programming language
- Difficulty
- Experience
- Interview language
- Previous answers
- Resume where available
- Job description where available

11. ADAPTIVE AI INTERVIEW
==================================================

This is one of the most important features.

The interview must adapt to performance.

After each answer:

1. Evaluate the answer.
2. Identify strengths.
3. Identify weaknesses.
4. Identify topic.
5. Determine performance level.
6. Generate the next question accordingly.

WEAK ANSWER:
Ask foundational or easier follow-up questions.

AVERAGE ANSWER:
Ask practical/application questions.

STRONG ANSWER:
Increase difficulty.

EXCELLENT ANSWER:
Ask deeper scenario-based or advanced questions.

Do not repeat questions.

Maintain interview context.

Store useful structured metadata only.

Do NOT store or expose hidden chain-of-thought.

Store:
- topic
- difficulty
- question type
- performance category
- next question strategy
